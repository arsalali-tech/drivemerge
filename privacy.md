# DriveMerge Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

## Overview

DriveMerge is a software application designed to help users manage available storage across multiple Google Drive accounts.

## Google Drive Data

DriveMerge may request access to Google Drive data necessary to provide its features. The specific access depends on the permissions requested by the application.

## Data Storage

DriveMerge is designed to keep users' files in their own Google Drive accounts. Any handling of account information, access tokens, file metadata, or other data depends on the application's actual implementation.

## Data Sharing

DriveMerge does not intend to sell users' personal information. Any data sharing must be consistent with the application's actual practices and applicable requirements.

## Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect information handled by DriveMerge.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the DriveMerge team through its designated support channel.
